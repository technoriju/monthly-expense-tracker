Privacy Policy – Khoroch

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Applies to the Khoroch app (Android package com.rijukarar.expensetracker)

In short: Khoroch keeps your income, expenses, budgets and settings only on your phone. There is no account and no server for your data, and the developer never receives it. The free Android app shows banner ads from Google AdMob; Google's ad SDK collects some device data to serve those ads. Your financial data is never shared with Google or anyone else.

1. Who we are

Khoroch ("the app") is developed by Riju Karar ("we", "the developer"), India. Contact: technoriju@gmail.com.

2. Data stored only on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. We cannot see or access it.

  • Transactions you enter: amount, income/expense type, category, date, optional note and payment method, and, for entries added by voice, the text you reviewed and saved.
  • Budgets and categories.
  • Preferences: app language, voice language, theme, currency and app-lock setting.
  • Optional profile: the name you type and a profile photo you choose.

The app does not encrypt this data itself; it relies on your phone's screen lock and storage protection. On Android, the app's data is excluded from Google's automatic cloud backup. On iOS, a device backup (iCloud or computer) may include app data, depending on your device settings.

3. Voice entry and the microphone

Voice entry is optional. When you use it, the app uses your microphone and your phone's speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS) to turn speech into text.

  • Depending on your device and language, recognition happens on the device or the audio is sent to that provider. The provider's own privacy policy applies to that processing.
  • The app does not record or store audio. It keeps only the text you review and save.
  • Every voice entry is shown to you for review before it is saved.
  • You can use the app fully without voice, and you can deny or revoke microphone access in your phone's settings at any time.

4. Profile photo

If you choose a profile photo, you pick it with your phone's photo picker. The app copies only that one photo into its private storage. It does not read your other photos, does not upload the photo, and does not include it in backups.

5. App lock

If you turn on app lock, the app asks your phone's operating system to check your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password. The app only receives a "success" or "failure" result. It never sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or screen-lock code.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob, Android)

The Dashboard and Reports screens show a banner ad from Google AdMob. Ads never appear on the screens where you add or edit entries, record by voice or review entries.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • app and ad interactions, such as app launches and ad taps;
  • diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the ad SDK.

This data is encrypted in transit and processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Never shared with Google or any ad network: your transactions, amounts, categories, notes, budgets, voice text, profile, backups or settings.

Your choices:

  • Where consent is required by law (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message before ads are requested. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • If ads cannot load (for example when offline), the app keeps working normally.

The iOS version of the app does not show ads.

7. Update check

When the app starts, and when you return to it (at most every 15 minutes), it downloads a small public file from expensetracker.dlyneed.com that lists the current and supported app versions. The request contains no personal data, identifiers, transactions or settings. As with any web request, the server can see your IP address. If your version is no longer supported, the app asks you to update from the store; your data is not changed or deleted.

8. Backups and exports

Backup (JSON) and CSV export files are created only when you ask. They contain your transactions, budgets, categories and basic settings, are not encrypted, and go only where you choose to save or share them (for example a folder, a cloud drive or a messaging app). The app itself never uploads them. Keep them somewhere safe.

9. Contacting support

The Help & Support screen can open your email app or WhatsApp to contact us. The app sends nothing by itself. We receive only what you choose to send, use it only to answer you, and do not share it. Your email or WhatsApp provider's privacy policy applies to that message.

10. Data sharing and sale

  • We do not sell your data.
  • Your financial data is not shared with the developer or any third party.
  • The only third-party data collection is the device data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK described in section 6.
  • The app has no analytics or tracking SDK of its own and does not log your amounts, notes or voice text.

11. Permissions

  • Microphone (and Speech Recognition on iOS): only for voice entry, only while you record.
  • Biometrics / Face ID: only if you turn on app lock.
  • Photos: only through the system photo picker, when you choose a profile photo.
  • Internet: used by the update check, the system speech service and the Google Mobile Ads SDK. None of your financial data is sent.
  • Advertising ID (Android): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show and measure ads.

12. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Because we never hold your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

  • Settings → Clear all data deletes all transactions and budgets in the app.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device (except copies in your device's own system backup, if any).
  • Backup or export files you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete them separately.
  • To limit ad data, use the ad privacy options and advertising-ID controls in section 6. Requests about data Google holds go to Google.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Your app data is fully under your control on your device. For any privacy question or request, email technoriju@gmail.com; we aim to reply within 30 days.

14. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

15. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
